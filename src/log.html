Experiment log

The results that did not become claims. Each entry is a hypothesis, what happened, and the verdict, dated. Kept because the negative results are where most of the learning was, and because a portfolio that only shows wins is not a portfolio, it is an advertisement.

Verdicts: killed the idea was dropped; kept it shipped; finding a fact we now design around; inconclusive not enough evidence either way.

2026-08Fantasy Premier Leaguekilled

Balanced sample weights will help the predictor on high-scoring players

Weighting training rows so that rare haulers count as much as blanks. Per-gameweek rank correlation fell from 0.747 to 0.662 and mean absolute error rose from 0.825 to 0.902. A Tweedie loss tried in the same session raised correlation by 0.0016 and worsened every error bin. Both rejected; the plain MAE objective stayed.

2026-08Fantasy Premier Leaguekept

Player-prop betting odds carry information the other features lack

Adding bookmaker anytime-scorer and assist odds cut mean absolute error from 0.9147 to 0.8914 (−2.5%) on eight held-out gameweeks and raised per-gameweek rank correlation in all eight (+0.0076 on average). A small effect that passed a sign test; shipped, at the cost of a paid data feed.

2026-07Phantomfinding

Phantom's signal is largely the low-volatility anomaly

A naive sort on inverse trailing volatility has a higher raw rank IC (0.165 against 0.124) and is 0.63 rank-correlated with the model's forecast. Phantom's independent contribution is significant only inside the mid- and high-volatility terciles (spread t = 2.6 and 4.1); combining the two signals gives IC 0.157. Found during the July audit; now the first line of the README's limitations.

2026-06Polymarket, June rewritekilled

Centring maker quotes on an options-implied fair value should improve a market-making strategy

A fair value from Deribit's implied-volatility surface beat the market's own Brier score on 2.9 million observations (0.0631 against 0.0643). In an event-driven maker simulation, centring quotes on it hurt at every weight tried, down to 5%. The shipped maker quotes around the book mid and uses the model only for risk. Two bugs were found on the way: a double-counted trade feed (mirror legs) and a timestamp-unit error that voided one full simulation version.

2026-06Polymarket research memoinconclusive

A Deribit-anchored fair value prices Polymarket's daily crypto ladders better than the market

Pre-registered gates over 1,304 markets from April to June. On daily "above" ladders the model was indistinguishable from the market mid (Brier difference −0.0012, 95% interval −0.0033 to +0.0008); on 4-hour up/down markets the market was clearly better (+0.0119). A taker-side test found a NO-side edge of 18 to 19 cents a contract on 71 to 108 trades, which the memo itself attributes to a launch-window effect. Not pursued.

2026-05Polymarket, favourite pressfinding

Where the live edge actually was

Reconciling 3,037 live trades against on-chain fills: trades where the two asks summed to between 1.00 and 1.02 made $521 (n = 2,070, z = 2.39) and every wider-spread bucket lost; entries 10 to 15 minutes before settlement made money, entries 5 to 10 minutes before did not; ETH lost $181 over 718 trades. The reconciliation also cut the dashboard's +$549 to +$383 by removing phantom partial fills. My collaborator's analysis.

2026-04Polymarket, S7killed

An at-the-money strike strategy on hourly markets has a 98.8% win rate

It did, with the settlement-time price used to choose the strike. With the entry-time price: 94.5% win rate, +0.52% return on stake, per-trade Sharpe 0.85 instead of 9.5, over 696 trades. Never traded. Every backtest now reads market data through one accessor with an explicit as-of time. Full postmortem.

2026-04Polymarket, S5 on 5-minute marketskilled

The favourite-press rule transfers from 15-minute to 5-minute markets

Backtests said yes. Live: 216 trades, 71.3% win rate, −$494, at entry prices where 71% is below break-even. Thinner books and worse fills than the snapshot backtest assumed. Cut entirely; the 15-minute variant was tightened at the same time. Full postmortem.

2026-04Polymarket, S6 machine-learned gatekilled

A LightGBM expected-value gate improves the favourite press on 5-minute markets

Walk-forward it showed 81.0% win rate. Live on 5-minute markets: 288 trades, +$4.61, ETH below break-even. XRP was dropped from the gate for miscalibration and the gate was moved to the 15-minute cadence. Calibrated where it traded, flat in practice.

2026-04Polymarket, S1 streak reversalfinding

Taker execution destroys a small edge

After three to five same-direction 5-minute outcomes the next reverses 53 to 57% of the time. As a maker, 2,745 out-of-sample trades make $64; as a taker the identical trades lose $95, because the crypto taker fee was 7.2%. Every configuration tested had the same sign flip. The design rule became: post-only or nothing.

2026-04Polymarket, sizing studyfinding

Sizing rules cannot manufacture Sharpe

Walk-forward over 8,726 markets: half-Kelly sizing multiplied profit and drawdown by 2.5 and left the per-trade Sharpe at 0.38; rule-based size tilts lowered it to 0.34; the production entry filter alone had raised it from 0.27 to 0.38, and a logistic expected-value gate reached 0.41 with a third fewer trades. Edge comes from selection, not from position size.

2026-04Polymarket, first-generation strategieskilled

An Avellaneda-Stoikov market maker with a toxicity regime switch can make money on multi-outcome markets

Eleven live days from 28 March: the market maker lost $168.64, the dispersion trade and the tail position roughly broke even. All three retired on 9 April when the favourite-press strategies replaced them.

2026-04Phantom on Polymarketkilled

Mid-price limit orders capture the same edge as paying the ask

Same signals, mid-price fills instead of the ask: win rate fell from 67% to 54% and the backtest ended at $787 instead of $4,714. Adverse selection: the orders that fill are the ones the market has already moved against. Also killed in the same study: ETH range markets (36% win rate over 807 signals), sizing above 2% of capital (3% went bankrupt), and active take-profit/stop-loss management, which lost while holding to settlement made money.

2026-04Phantomkilled

Pretraining on synthetic stochastic-process paths transfers to real markets

Versions 1 and 2 reached the oracle CRPS on synthetic data (0.064 against a theoretical floor of 0.065) and transferred nothing: on real BTC the fine-tuned model's CRPS matched a post-hoc Gaussian. Version 3 on real multi-asset data gave calibrated uncertainty and zero directional signal (IC 0.00); version 4 on absolute multi-horizon returns memorised training directions. The signal appeared only when the target became the cross-sectionally demeaned return (version 5).

2026-04Phantomkilled

Funding rates, taker-buy ratios and 4-hour bars add information

Funding-rate and taker-buy features changed IC by −0.0014 and −0.0005; the gain attributed to that version came from training on crypto only. Four-hour bars lowered 10-day IC from 0.140 to 0.095 because adjacent windows share 99.7% of their bars. Widening the universe from 60 to 362 coins (version 8) lowered mean IC slightly and diversified the long-short portfolio, which is a breadth effect, not a better model.

2026-04IMC Prosperity 4killed

Four signals that did not survive an overfitting audit

An option-implied drift signal (+4,716 in-sample) had a train-to-holdout correlation of −0.700 across 26 random-search configurations and beat the baseline on both in none. A cross-strike spread (+11,265) was a mid-to-mid accounting artefact: 94 of 94 configurations were non-positive under realistic fills. Counterparty identifiers lost in all nine variants. A round-5 overlay was shown to be a no-op by byte-identical trade histories across three submissions. Also: the portal's 1,000-tick preview backtest is noise; our shipped round-3 trader showed +17,449 there and made +11,141 over the full 10,000 ticks.

2026-04Fantasy Premier Leaguekept

The game's own expected-points field is a post-match leak

A contributor's report argued the feature was contaminated and that a 3,376-point season built on it was invalid. Capturing the field live mid-gameweek for players who had already scored heavily showed it frozen at its deadline value, and reproducing its formula matched 426 of 429 live values (99.3%). The feature was kept and is the most important one in the production model. The disputed season score was retired anyway when the honest replay harness replaced it.

2026-04Reliability-engineering LLMinconclusive

GRPO on the fine-tuned model improves reasoning on hard questions

On 266 questions the base model solves inconsistently, GRPO from the fine-tuned checkpoint reached 57.9% at step 80 against 50.8%, McNemar p = 0.056, then declined to 54.1% and 52.3% with more steps. On a 54-question independent holdout neither GRPO variant moved the needle (p above 0.8). Led by my co-author. Suggestive, not settled.

2026-04Reliability-engineering LLMfinding

Paraphrases teach more than hard problems, and cross-validation flatters

Sixty-five harder generated questions moved accuracy by +2.0 points (p = 0.69); 586 paraphrases with frozen numbers moved it by +18.6 (p = 0.002). The folds were shuffled at the question level, so paraphrases leak across the split; on an independent 54-question holdout the headline adapter scored 55.6% against the base model's 59.3%, and an adapter trained on a 600-question subset fell to 33.3% against 53.7%. Grouped folds are the next experiment.

2026-03Reliability-engineering LLMkilled

Direct preference optimisation and an early GRPO beat supervised fine-tuning

DPO on 215 questions: −1.7 points. GRPO with a judge-model reward through TRL: +0.5. The same month, the +7.0-point supervised result from an earlier run was re-evaluated with greedy decoding and became +2.3; every experiment since uses greedy decoding.

2026-03Reliability-engineering LLMfinding

Fine-tuning a thinking model on non-thinking solutions collapses its reasoning

All fine-tuned responses in the first fold had empty thinking blocks; answers were eight times shorter than the base model's (679 against 5,489 characters) and 9.3% looped. Thinking mode was disabled for training and evaluation thereafter.

2026-02DeFi Sentinel (HackEurope)killed

A rug-pull classifier with AUC 0.9995 is the one to ship

The best-scoring model drew 89% of its importance from deployer-history features that are unavailable at the moment a new token is scanned. The team shipped a model restricted to 77 features that exist at scan time (AUC 0.999 on a temporal split, with over half of its importance in one metadata-completeness feature). An audit of the public dataset's labels had also found that 67.4% of tokens labelled legitimate had had 95% or more of their liquidity drained, so the labels themselves were noisy. Hackathon-scale evidence, stated as such.
