Builder-researcher: I take a prediction problem from data to a model to something running, then hold the result to the same standard as the experiment.

I am finishing an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at CentraleSupélec in Paris and spending the final months of it as an applied AI research intern at Siemens in Munich. Before the MSc I studied mathematics and computer science at Boston College, and spent two summers doing alpha research at a systematic equity hedge fund in London. Outside those, I co-built and ran a live prediction-market trading system, trained a distributional forecaster for cryptocurrencies, fine-tuned an 8B language model for reliability engineering, and placed 458th of 18,803 teams in IMC's Prosperity 4 trading competition. The front page lists all of it with dates and results.

Two things I care about: research that ends up running (the point of a forecast is a decision), and results that survive their own audit. I use Claude Code heavily and deliberately, with custom skills in most of my repositories, which is a large part of how one person ships a production rewrite in a few days.
