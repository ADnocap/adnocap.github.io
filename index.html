Numbers I have retracted

Every result above ships with its caveat, and some shipped with a mistake first. These are the ones I found and corrected, with the date. The experiment log has the negative results that never became claims.

  • 2026-07 Sharpe 13.0→≈ 4.1, gross, an upper bound Phantom's long-short series consisted of overlapping 10-day returns sampled daily, and I had annualised them as if they were daily returns, inflating the Sharpe ratio by about √10. README, paper draft, experiment log and evaluation code were corrected; net of realistic costs I consider 1 to 2 plausible.
  • 2026-05 +$549→+$383 over 18 days The trading dashboard counted phantom partial fills. Reconciling every trade against the on-chain record cut the favourite-press strategy's live result by 30%. The reconciliation was my collaborator's work.
  • 2026-04 Sharpe 9.5, 98.8% win rate→Sharpe 0.85, 94.5% A backtest for an at-the-money strike strategy picked the strike using the settlement-time price instead of the entry-time price. Fifteen minutes of look-ahead was the entire edge.
  • 2026-03 +7.0 pp→+2.3 pp An early fine-tuning gain on the reliability-engineering benchmark was evaluated with temperature sampling. Re-evaluated with greedy decoding, most of it was sampling variance. Every later experiment uses greedy decoding.
